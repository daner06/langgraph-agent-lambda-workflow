# Nexara Daniel Ltd - FY2026 Internal Financial Planning Assumptions

**Date:** June 2026  
**Classification:** Internal - Finance & Strategy (UK)  
**Owner:** FP&A Team

## Executive Summary
This document sets out the key planning assumptions for the FY2026 annual operating plan and board budget for Nexara Daniel Ltd, a UK-headquartered AI infrastructure and platform company. Assumptions are reviewed quarterly and include base and downside scenarios reflecting UK energy costs, data residency requirements, and enterprise demand in the UK and EU.

## Segment Revenue Projections (ARR, GBP millions)

| Segment                    | FY2025A | FY2026 Plan | YoY Growth | Notes |
|----------------------------|---------|-------------|------------|-------|
| Enterprise Platform        | 312     | 398         | +28%       | New verticals (manufacturing, energy, public sector) |
| Developer & SMB            | 98      | 127         | +30%       | Self-serve + improved trial conversion |
| Professional Services      | 41      | 48          | +17%       | Large AI workflow implementations |
| **Total ARR**              | **451** | **573**     | **+27%**   | Base case; range GBP 555M - GBP 590M |

## Gross Margin & Unit Economics
- Blended gross margin target: 70.5% (vs 68.8% FY2025)
- Enterprise subscription gross margin: 74%
- Key drivers: 18% reduction in per-token inference cost from internal model optimisations + shift of 22% of inference traffic to the new Manchester cluster (lower power cost vs previous colocation).

## Operating Expense Assumptions (as % of revenue)
- R&D: 26% (focus on inference optimisation and UK/EU vertical solutions)
- Sales & Marketing: 21% (improved self-serve efficiency)
- G&A: 7.5%

## UK-Specific Considerations
- Data residency: Majority of new enterprise workloads require UK or EU data centre locations.
- Energy: Planning assumes continued access to renewable PPAs; sensitivity of +15% on UK power prices would reduce gross margin by ~1.4 pp.
- Enterprise sales cycle in UK public sector and regulated industries remains longer but higher ACV.

## Scenario Summary (FY2026 ARR, GBP)
- Base: GBP 573M
- Downside A (mild UK/EU macro slowdown): GBP 535M
- Downside B (delayed Manchester cluster + slower enterprise AI adoption): GBP 498M
